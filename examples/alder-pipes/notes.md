## Pulls found in the chapters

- Westland at 46% of revenue against a smallest new lot of €60 million. Kept: both sides filed. Who pays: the Westland crews and Alder Holding.
- Paying crews monthly against being paid after acceptance, on a larger lot. Kept. Who pays: Alder Holding's cash.
- A second welding crew while no board has tendered the work for it. Removed: any builder preparing for a program does this, so a competitor's name in its place keeps it true.
- Debts up while revenue rose. Removed: the Sector rules chapter reads those debts as supplier bills on open sections.
