# The north star

A person names a company and gives its website. At the end they hold two things they can read in half an hour: ten chapters of facts about how the company works, and one page that says what is going on there and what decision it faces.

## The world view

Ten chapters, one Markdown file each. A chapter rebuilds how one part of the company works, from the evidence alone. It carries facts, each with its source and date. It carries no conclusion and no advice. The person can open any chapter and follow any fact back to the page it came from.

The chapters come first, and the reading is cut from them. When the reading and a chapter disagree on a fact, the chapter wins: fix the chapter if it is wrong, then fix the reading.

[chapters.md](chapters.md) has the ten chapters and their form.

## The first reading

One page in eight parts. It reads like a colleague explaining the company over dinner: the market, the rules of the trade, who works with whom, what the company has and what limits it, where the facts pull against each other, and the decision in front of a named body, with the options and what each costs.

A good reading lets the company's own people say: "Yes, we all know this, and nobody has written it down." It describes the system and leaves the choice to the people who own it.

[reading.md](reading.md) has the eight parts and how to write them.

## The five rules

1. **Every figure has a source and a date.** A figure without both stays out of the chapters and the reading.
2. **Name every actor.** Write the firm, the buyer, the regulator, the body that decides. Never "the vendor", "the regulator" or "the market leader" without the name.
3. **Describe the company and score nothing.** No grades, no stars, no scores, no advice. Write the company as a thing that acts: it has no wishes, fears or gender.
4. **Each finding names who pays.** Every pull between two facts says who carries its cost. Every option says what it costs and who carries that cost. The one who pays can be a third party: the staff, the owners, the buyers, a partner.
5. **A person checks the reading before it reaches anyone outside the run.** The second model is part of the run. The reading's status line records the check.

A fact is any sentence with a figure, a date, a name or a cause. A finding is a pull in part 6 or an option in part 7 of the reading.

## How to write

- One idea per sentence. At most one figure per sentence.
- Explain each mechanism in the words you would say aloud. "The board pays after it accepts a section, so the builder carries four to six months of wages first."
- Keep every hedge: "may", "about", "if". An inference stays an inference.
- State a known gap once, where it first matters.
- Where the figures say the company is fine, say it is fine.
- The person's own writing rules in `setup.md` come on top.
